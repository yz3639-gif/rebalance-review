REBALANCE REVIEW · METHODOLOGY & PRIVACY · VERSION 1.2

Understand what your review means.

This is a hypothetical comparison of current weights, not your actual account performance, a forecast or a recommendation. Generated examples are clearly labeled synthetic.

Inputs and coverage

Holdings and prices are separate inputs. Supply percentages or USD market values; market-value coverage is unknown when you supply only weights. Search the versioned US-listed ETF directory by code or name. Up to 50 distinct noncash holdings across both portfolios, plus USD cash, may be compared. Directory inclusion does not guarantee usable provider history, USD currency or a verified adjustment convention. Other positions stay visible and require confirmation before a partial comparison.

Use adjusted-close or total-return-index prices in USD. Dates must be real, ordered and continuous over the common period. The US session calendar is explicitly bounded to 1990–2028. Missing observations, conflicting duplicates and invalid prices block analysis; no filling or interpolation occurs. Provider adjustment calculations are declared by the user, not independently certified.

Use CASH, USD CASH or USD_CASH for modeled cash. USD by itself is the ProShares Ultra Semiconductors ETF and is treated as a risky fund, never cash.

Risk estimates

The default window uses 252 daily returns, requiring 253 price observations. Additional 126- and 504-return estimates test window sensitivity; unavailable windows are shown as unavailable. Both portfolios use the same union of covered assets and common dates.

Centered Ledoit–Wolf covariance is fit to risky assets with maximum-likelihood normalization and annualized by 252. Cash is appended with an exact-zero covariance row and column. Signed Euler contributions sum to total estimated volatility. Negative contributions are valid diversification effects; they are not clipped. All-cash portfolios have zero volatility and no defined relative risk contributions. Correlation is not a measurement of overlapping ETF constituents.

Historical replay

History is limited to the latest five calendar years of common prices. Initial capital buys the selected allocation at the first observation, including purchase fees. Monthly or quarterly targets are signaled at period end and executed at the next supplied session close; existing holdings earn the execution-day return. Buy and hold purchases once. A target without a later execution date remains unfilled. No forced liquidation occurs at the end.

Fees apply to risky-asset purchases and sales, not cash transfers. The default cost is 5 basis points per side (0.05%), with 2/5/10/20-basis-point sensitivity and a one-extra-session delay check. Cash earns exactly 0% only after confirmation. The ledger solves for post-fee wealth so holdings plus cash and fees reconcile. Adjusted prices already reflect distributions and fund expenses; these are not charged twice.

Total return and maximum drawdown include the initial fee. Annualized historical volatility uses subsequent daily returns, excluding the initial purchase event. Turnover is cumulative gross buys plus sells divided by pre-trade wealth. No reliable risk-free benchmark is supplied, so the main report does not show Sharpe ratios.

What remains unanswered

Historical relationships can change. The tool does not determine your objectives, risk tolerance or tax position. It does not model deposits, withdrawals, leverage, live broker execution or your historical trades. Cost assumptions are scenarios, not quotes. No probability of loss or opaque risk score is provided.

Privacy and permission

Calculations run locally in a Web Worker. Local CSV files, holdings and reasons stay in your browser. Optional Tiingo requests transmit your ticker list, dates and user-owned key through the same-origin restricted proxy. Customer REST APIs are contacted directly from your browser. Keys are held in memory and excluded from reports and archives; the app has no analytics, account, shared API key or brokerage connection. It loads its own executable assets; warmed assets remain in memory for offline recalculation in the current tab. Cold offline startup is not supported.

Saving is opt-in through browser IndexedDB. Reviews remain on this device; clearing site data removes them. Export permitted archives for backup. No scheduled notification is created by a next-review date. A new review clears old market data and requires fresh input, while the previous report remains a separate snapshot.

Each dataset carries display, raw-storage, derived-storage, export and public-display permissions. Unknown rights do not imply permission. Raw retention, derived retention and full-report export are separate permissions. Tiingo defaults to transient prices and no derived-result export or storage. Restricted sources can produce a clearly labeled decision-only PDF with your own inputs and reasoning, excluding market-derived results. Optional raw data is included in archives only when separately permitted. Imported archives are user-controlled: structural checks are not a signature or independent reproduction of their calculations.

Cash and downloadable reports

Both portfolios may be entirely USD cash without an ETF file. Choose a period with at least 253 verified sessions and confirm the zero-return assumption; wealth is constant and relative risk contributions are not applicable. One-click PDF freezes the report at click time, embeds locally hosted fonts, and includes all permitted report sections. Chinese reasoning is supported. New reviews cancel pending work and clear unsaved inputs and keys while preserving the saved journal.

Validation status

The repository contains independent Python numerical fixtures, TypeScript property/invariant tests and production-build browser tests. Passing automated checks is not proof of profitable investing or universal accessibility. Tests with five real data owners, a twelve-person competitor comparison, four-week repeat use and real-phone checks require actual participant/device evidence and remain separate release gates.

Local market history without an API key

When started with npm start, fresh local reviews use Yahoo Finance daily adjusted history automatically. Enter two allocations totaling 100% and choose Compare portfolios. Each requested ETF is checked for identity, USD currency, adjusted prices and complete trading sessions. The report uses the previous trading session as its requested cutoff; a recently launched holding shortens the common history.

This unofficial connection is for personal research on this computer. Requested symbols and dates go to Yahoo; weights, starting capital and reasons stay in the browser. Prices are released after calculation. Local result saving, JSON archives and full analytical PDF export are disabled for this source; a decision-only PDF remains available. Provider errors never substitute synthetic data. CSV and your own API remain available, and public deployment does not enable this local connection.
